## 🔍 2. Technical Weak Topic Audit
1. What concept confused me the most this week?
2. Can I explain it without looking at notes? If NO, schedule a 60-min revision slot for Monday morning.
3. Did I write tests for my code?
4. What was the most critical bug I debugged this week?

---

## 💼 3. Career & Pipeline Audit
1. Which job applications got viewed or responded to?
2. Are my resume keywords matching the job descriptions (FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Docker)?
3. What feedback did I get on freelance proposals?
4. What is the #1 objection OneBridge Social demo users gave? How do I fix it this week?
